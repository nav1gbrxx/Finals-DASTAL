# Nav1Gate and Inventory Presentation Outline

## 1. Title and group

Introduce the project title, members, subject, and section.

## 2. Real-world problem

Explain customer routing, waiting time, staff availability, repairs, assembly assistance,
retail assistance, and PC-parts stock.

## 3. System objectives

Describe FIFO waitlists, sector-specific staff assignment, service completion history,
accurate inventory, and structure demonstrations.

## 4. User interface

Demonstrate the dashboard metrics and sector queues, registering a customer,
assigning the next customer, completing service, editing the staff roster, and
managing PC parts.

## 5. UML design

Present `main`, `InventorySystem`, `Product`, storage, and data structure relationships.

## 6. Data structure demonstration

- Array/List: primary product and staff collections
- Linked List: service history
- Stack: active/finished service history and undo
- Queue: sector-specific customer waitlists
- BST: price-ordered view
- Hash Table: SKU and ticket lookup
- Set: unique service sectors

## 7. Algorithm demonstration

- Linear search
- Binary search
- Merge sort
- Divide-and-conquer nearest-price search

## 8. Live application

Register a customer, assign sector staff, complete the ticket, add a staff member,
create/update/delete a part, search, and process a restock.

## 9. Testing

Present `test-report.md` and the passing automated test command.

## 10. Conclusion

Summarize learning outcomes and future features such as barcode support, role-based
access, or multi-user synchronization.
